Varanasi, woven in gold.

Varanasi, also called Banaras, sits on the Ganga and is known for its silk brocade. Banarasi sarees carry gold and silver zari in borders, buttis and all-over jaal, the same motifs you can see on every saree in this catalog.

Bloom Banarasi shares that tradition in colours made for weddings, festivals and everyday celebration. Colours and availability change often, so message us to see what is new.
